{% call panel('Start with the correct PTE test') %}

Different PTE tests serve different purposes. Check the exact test accepted for your application before choosing preparation or paying for an examination.

  • PTE Academic: Intended for academic English assessment and accepted by participating institutions.
  • PTE Academic UKVI: Check whether your application specifically requires this version.
  • PTE Core: Assesses general English and serves different application purposes from PTE Academic. Do not assume that it is interchangeable with Academic for university admission.

Use Pearson’s information about PTE Academic and PTE Core alongside the requirements published by your receiving institution or authority.

{% endcall %} {% call panel('Computer-based practice with a purpose') %}

PTE preparation involves more than knowing vocabulary. You also need to understand task instructions, organize a response and work within the relevant time limits.

Visa Culture provides unlimited practice on its own preparation platform. Ask us about account access, classroom arrangements and any equipment you need before joining.

Use each attempt to identify a specific improvement rather than repeating questions without reviewing your responses.

{% endcall %} {% call panel('Connect practice across the four skills') %}

Speaking

Practise producing clear, relevant spoken responses. Pay attention to pronunciation, fluency and completing the task rather than relying on memorized answers that may not fit the question.

Writing

Work on sentence accuracy, organization and responding to instructions. Review the grammar and vocabulary choices that affect how clearly your meaning is expressed.

Reading

Develop your understanding of written passages, sentence relationships and context. Practise selecting answers based on meaning rather than matching individual words.

Listening

Practise following spoken information and identifying important details. Build a review habit so that missed information becomes a focus for your next attempt.

The task types you practise should match your chosen PTE test. Pearson publishes separate format guides for Academic and Academic UKVI and Core.

{% endcall %} {% call panel('Timed mock tests and individual feedback') %}

Timed mock tests help you practise pacing and recognize tasks that need more attention. Individual feedback supports focused work on the responses and English skills you are developing.

Visa Culture’s practice platform should not be confused with Pearson’s official scored practice products. Pearson describes its own Scored Practice Tests as using its official scoring engine. Explore Pearson’s official preparation products.

Practice performance does not guarantee an official score, admission or a visa outcome.
