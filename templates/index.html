Academic Qualifications

  • • Undergraduate: Minimum 60–70% in HSSC/Intermediate
  • • Postgraduate: Minimum 60–65% in Bachelor's degree
  • • Top universities may require 70–80%

English Language Proficiency

Minimum requirement: IELTS overall score 6.0–6.5 (or equivalent)

  • • IELTS – minimum 6.0–6.5 overall
  • • PTE Academic
  • • TOEFL iBT
  • • Cambridge C1 Advanced
  • • Test results must be no more than two years old

Student Visa – Subclass 500

Allows full-time study at a registered institution with part-time work up to 48 hours per fortnight during term time.

  • • Valid Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from CRICOS-registered institution
  • • Genuine Student Requirement – demonstrate primary intent is education
  • • Financial capacity proof – minimum annual living cost: AUD 29,710
  • • Visa fee: AUD 1,600–2,000
  • • Processing time: 4–8 weeks

Health & Compliance

  • • Medical examination with approved panel doctor required
  • • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) mandatory for entire stay
  • • Police Clearance Certificate for character requirements
  • • Maintain CRICOS-registered course enrollment

Post-Study Work Opportunity

After graduation, eligible graduates can apply for a Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) to work in Australia for 2–4 years, depending on qualification level. This provides valuable international work experience and potential pathways to permanent residency.

Cost Reduction Strategies

  • • Explore scholarship opportunities and financial aid programs
  • • Engage in part-time work during studies (up to 48 hours per fortnight during term)
  • • Consider studying in more affordable cities like Adelaide or Brisbane
  • • Look into pathway programs for reduced tuition entry
