Frequently Asked

Common questions about studying in Sydney

Everything you need to know about admissions, scholarships, visa, and life in Australia.

What IELTS/PTE scores do I need for Sydney?

Most programs ask for IELTS 6.5 overall with at least 6.0 in each band. Some competitive streams require 7.0. PTE is accepted with equivalent scores. We provide target scores for each course during counseling.

When should I apply for intake?

International applications are commonly planned around Semester 1 and Semester 2 entry windows. Applying early helps with offers, accommodation, and visa planning.

How much does it cost to live in Sydney while studying at UNSW?

Monthly living costs are usually around AUD 1,500-2,000 for rent, food, transport, and utilities. With scholarships and part-time work, many students manage a comfortable lifestyle.

Can international students work while studying?

Work rights depend on current Australian student visa conditions. Always verify the latest official rules before travel.

What is the Genuine Student Requirement?

The Genuine Student requirement (introduced in 2024, replacing the previous GTE requirement) assesses whether you are genuinely intending to study. It examines your academic background, course choice, career goals, financial situation, and personal circumstances. A strong, personalized statement showing your genuine intent is critical for visa approval.
