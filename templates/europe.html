Admissions

Check your academic qualifications

Ask how the institution assesses Pakistani HSSC, A-levels or previous degrees. Check whether you need certified translations, subject prerequisites or a credential evaluation.

Language

Confirm the teaching language

Check the language of the entire degree and what proof of proficiency the institution accepts. For placements, ask whether the local language is required.

Tuition

Compare tuition for international students

Many German public programmes charge little or no tuition, though exceptions and semester contributions apply. Countries such as Hungary and Sweden generally charge tuition to non-EU degree students. Use the exact programme fee in your budget. European tuition guide

The Schengen Area

Study in one country and visit others.

All six destinations on this page are in the Schengen Area. If you hold a valid long-stay visa or residence permit issued by a Schengen country, you can generally visit other Schengen countries for short stays, subject to entry conditions. European Commission Schengen guide

Plan trips during study breaks

A student based in Hungary could visit Austria or Germany during a break. Check entry conditions and carry your passport and valid visa or residence permit.

Count 90 days in any 180-day period

For short visits, count the days you spend across other Schengen countries together. European Commission short-stay calculator

Apply through your study destination

For a degree or other long stay, follow the visa and residence rules of the country where you will study.

Check rules before studying or working elsewhere

A second country can require its own authorisation for work, study or a longer stay. If you plan an exchange, ask your institution whether student mobility rules apply. EU guidance on moving between countries

Student visas and arrival

Check the rules for your destination.

Pakistani applicants should confirm long-stay study requirements with the destination’s consulate or immigration authority. The required visa or permit and the order of steps vary by country. EU Immigration Portal
