FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score is usually needed for Melbourne?

Most programs require around IELTS 6.5 overall with band conditions depending on faculty.

Can Pakistani students apply for scholarships at offer stage?

Many scholarships are automatic with admission, while others need separate applications and deadlines.

How much does it cost to live in Melbourne as a student?

Living costs vary by housing style, but students generally budget for rent, transport, food, utilities, and insurance.

Can I work part-time while studying?

Yes, international students can work under current student visa work-limit conditions.
