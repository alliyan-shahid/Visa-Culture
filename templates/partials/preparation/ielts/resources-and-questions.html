Are your IELTS classes held in Sheikhupura?

Yes. Visa Culture offers in-person IELTS preparation at its Sheikhupura office.

Which IELTS tests do you prepare students for?

We prepare students for IELTS Academic, IELTS General Training and IELTS for UKVI. Confirm your required test before joining.

Do classes include mock tests and feedback?

Yes. Preparation includes timed mock tests and individual feedback across the skills you are developing.

Can I study spoken English separately?

Yes. General and spoken-English training is included in preparation and is also available separately on request.

How long will I need to prepare?

Preparation needs depend on your current English, target requirements and available study time. Contact us to discuss your circumstances and current class arrangements.

What IELTS score do I need?

Check your exact university, course and application requirements. There is no single IELTS score suitable for every institution or visa application.
