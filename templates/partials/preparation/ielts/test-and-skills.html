Before beginning preparation, check the exact test and scores required by your university or relevant authority. The overall score and individual skill requirements can differ between applications.

  • IELTS Academic: Commonly used for higher education. Preparation should match the Academic Reading and Writing tasks.
  • IELTS General Training: Uses different Reading and Writing tasks from Academic. Choose it only where it meets your application requirements.
  • IELTS for UKVI: Confirm the exact approved test required for your application before booking.

Academic and General Training share Listening and Speaking components, but their Reading and Writing components differ. Use the official IELTS test-type guidance to understand the options.

Listening

Practise following spoken information, identifying key details and checking that your answer matches the question. Review missed answers to understand whether the difficulty was vocabulary, attention or interpreting the recording.

Reading

Work on finding information, understanding meaning and managing your time. Practise reading the instructions carefully rather than choosing an answer because it contains familiar words.

Writing

Focus on answering the task, organizing ideas and expressing them clearly. Use individual feedback to identify weaknesses in grammar, vocabulary and sentence structure.

Your practice should match the Writing tasks in the IELTS test you plan to take.

Speaking

Build confidence in developing answers and explaining your ideas. Work on clear pronunciation, vocabulary and fluency while giving responses that address the question.

General and spoken-English training is included in preparation and is also available separately on request.
