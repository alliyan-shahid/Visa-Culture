Which PTE tests do you prepare students for?

Visa Culture offers preparation for PTE Academic, PTE Academic UKVI and PTE Core.

Do classes include computer-based practice?

Yes. Practice takes place on a computer using Visa Culture’s own preparation platform.

Is practice unlimited?

Yes, unlimited practice is available on our platform. Contact us to confirm account access arrangements and what is included with your preparation.

Is Visa Culture’s platform Pearson’s official scoring platform?

It is Visa Culture’s own preparation platform. Pearson’s official practice products are separate resources available through Pearson.

Are classes held in person?

Yes. Classes are held at the Sheikhupura office.

Should I choose PTE Academic or PTE Core?

Choose the test required for your application. They have different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable. Check the receiving institution or authority’s requirements before booking.

What score will I achieve?

An official score cannot be guaranteed. Preparation and feedback help you identify weaknesses and practise more effectively.
