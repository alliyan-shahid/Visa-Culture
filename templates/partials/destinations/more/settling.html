Student immigration and arrival

Prepare for your destination’s entry process.

Confirm the current process with your institution and the destination’s immigration authority or consulate. Requirements can depend on your nationality, course length and institution.

Confirm which student permission you need

Singapore and Malaysia use student passes, while the UAE offers a student residence visa. Check the application route and whether your institution needs to start or sponsor the process.

Prepare your documents

Check requirements for your passport, admission letter, financial evidence, translations and any medical screening or insurance.

04

Check work conditions before accepting a job

Verify whether you can work during term time, take an internship or apply for a separate graduate permission after completing your course.
