Student visa and arrival

Prepare for your Student visa and arrival.

Check current eligibility, fees and documents before applying. Your university’s international student team can help with questions about your course and sponsorship. GOV.UK Student visa guide

01

Get your CAS

Meet the conditions of your offer from a licensed student sponsor so it can issue your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).

02

Prepare your financial evidence

Where required, show funds for the course fees listed on your CAS and the applicable living-cost amount. Check the evidence rules and how long you must hold the funds. GOV.UK financial requirements

03

Apply and check your documents

Have your passport and CAS ready, and budget for the visa fee and immigration health surcharge. Check whether you need a tuberculosis certificate from an approved clinic or an ATAS certificate for your course. GOV.UK document checklist

04

Get ready to travel and enrol

Check the start date of your visa, follow the instructions for accessing your eVisa, and confirm your housing and enrolment arrangements before travelling.

05

Follow your work conditions

Some full-time degree students may work up to 20 hours per week during term time. Check the conditions of your own visa and your university’s term dates before accepting work.
