Study in Canada

Why Canada

Find a program that fits your next step.

Compare universities, public colleges and polytechnics across Canada. Look at the credential, language of instruction, total cost and opportunities to gain experience.

01

Undergraduate degrees

Compare four-year bachelor’s programs, specialist degrees and transfer routes. Check prerequisites and how each institution assesses previous coursework.

02

Taught and research graduate programs

Compare course-based and thesis-based master’s degrees. Research programs may ask for a proposal, a supervisor or both.

03

College and polytechnic pathways

Explore diplomas, certificates and applied degrees. If you plan to transfer, confirm which credits are accepted. Check the exact program’s eligibility for a post-graduation work permit if that matters to you.

04

Support for international students

Ask about orientation, academic advising, health coverage, housing support and services for students adjusting to a new campus.

Education in Canada

Plan your application and budget.

Application routes vary by institution and province. Check whether you should apply directly or through a provincial service, and keep track of program and scholarship deadlines.

Admissions

Check your academic qualifications

Ask how the institution assesses Pakistani HSSC, A-levels or prior degrees. Check required subjects and grades, and whether you need transcripts, translations, references or a portfolio.

English

Confirm your language requirements

Check whether the program is taught in English or French. Each institution sets its accepted proficiency tests, minimum scores and exemption rules.

Tuition

Compare international fees by program

EduCanada reports 2026 annual average international university tuition of CAD 41,746 for undergraduates and CAD 24,028 for graduate students. Confirm your program’s tuition and compulsory fees. EduCanada tuition and cost guide

Funding

Look for scholarships early

Compare university entrance awards, departmental funding and graduate assistantships. Check whether you need a separate application and when funding decisions are made. EduCanada scholarship search
