Frequently Asked

Common questions about studying in ANU

Everything you need to know about ANU admissions, scholarships, visa requirements, and student life in Canberra.

What IELTS/PTE scores do I need for ANU?

English requirements vary by degree. ANU accepts IELTS, PTE, and other approved tests, but required scores differ by program. Always check the exact ANU course page before applying.

When should I apply for intake?

ANU international applications are commonly planned around Semester 1 and Semester 2 entry windows. Applying early helps with offer timelines, accommodation, and visa preparation.

How much does it cost to live in Canberra while studying at ANU?

Monthly living costs are usually around AUD 1,500-2,000 for rent, food, transport, and utilities. With scholarships and part-time work, many students manage a comfortable lifestyle.

Can I work while studying?

International student work rights depend on current Australian visa conditions. Always confirm the latest official rules before travel.

What is the Genuine Student Requirement?

The Genuine Student requirement (introduced in 2024, replacing the previous GTE requirement) assesses whether you are genuinely intending to study. It examines your academic background, course choice, career goals, financial situation, and personal circumstances. A strong, personalized statement showing your genuine intent is critical for visa approval.
