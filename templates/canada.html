Study permit and arrival

Prepare for your study permit and arrival.

Most international students need a Canadian study permit for a degree or diploma. Check the application instructions and the documents required for your circumstances. IRCC study permit guide

AdmissionGet your acceptance letter
PermitApply for your permit

01

Get accepted by a designated learning institution

Obtain a letter of acceptance from a designated learning institution (DLI). Check the school and program details before paying a deposit.

02

Check attestation requirements

Check whether you need a provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL or TAL). If you’re studying in Québec, check the Québec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ) rules and any exemptions. IRCC document checklist

03

Show how you’ll pay

Prepare evidence that you can cover tuition, living costs and transportation without relying on work in Canada. Québec sets separate financial requirements. IRCC proof of funds guide

04

Apply online and prepare to travel

Check whether you need biometrics, an immigration medical exam or extra documents. If you’re approved from outside Canada, bring your port of entry letter and the required travel documents so an officer can assess your entry and issue your permit.

05

Follow your work conditions

Eligible students can usually work off campus up to 24 hours per week during regular academic sessions. Check your permit conditions and the separate rules for school-approved placements. IRCC off-campus work rules
